FAQ

Questions from farms

How much does CALIBER+ cost?

Robot-as-a-Service starts at €26,000 per season (≈€1.9/kg of picked berries). Purchase: Lite (4 arms) from €55,000, Flagship (6 arms) from €80,000.

How fast does the robot pick?

Up to 17 kg/h per unit with 12–14 hours of daily autonomy. It harvests two rows simultaneously, berry by berry.

Does the machine damage the berries?

No: the silicone-and-vacuum gripper holds each berry with under 2 N of force. CALIBER+ has 0% crop loss versus about 5% for over-the-row harvesters.

When can a farm get a robot?

Pilot programs run in season 2027 (30/60/90 days), commercial launch is 2028. Pilot slots for 2027 are open now.
